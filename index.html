05  /  A Bit More About Me

If you've read this far, hi.

Here's a bit more on how I actually work, and who I am when I'm not working.

📸
How I Work

Form AND Function, for me, will always be paramount. I am a strategic and research driven designer. I enjoy both the conceptualization AND the creation, as long as what I'm making solves the problem of the brief. I often write my own copy and do my own voice overs (lol! this was a surprise talent but I actually quite enjoy it). I never miss a deadline, I'm always raising my hand to help on things outside my lane, and I'm happiest when I get to learn something new to pull a project off (see: teaching myself to build this kind of site).

I'm a zillenial who loves humor and having fun. I'm not precious about what I make, always open to feedback, and I love working with good people.

Outside of Work

I love interior design, health and wellness, and crafting. If I'm not working on a project at home you can find me sleuthing for gluten free bakeries, working out, or being manipulated by Wally (also known as Wallybear, Wallabus, or Bubbabear) to give him cheese (my favorite place to be.)

Wally is almost 6 — and a little reactive (covid pup! 🤪) so he may make himself known on calls, but he's a very good boy who uses buttons to speak, and is the prince of our DINKWAD home. We live in NJ outside of Philly, but are originally from Baltimore County Maryland.

How I Think About AI

I've spent 9+ years building real creative expertise across design, strategy, motion, and advertising. I believe AI is excellent for evening the playing field of creative ideas, allowing many to conceptualize and create at a much faster pace without barrier. But I think it's important to treat these tools just like Photoshop or After Effects. I loathe AI slop. Tasteful AI use is dependent on the creator. An art director's eye is more important than ever.
